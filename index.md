# PassManager privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

PassManager is an offline password manager. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## We collect nothing
The app has no account, no server and no internet access (it does not request the Android INTERNET
permission). It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code. We never receive any of your data.

## Your vault
Everything you store is encrypted on your phone with XChaCha20-Poly1305, using a key derived from your master
password with Argon2id. The vault is excluded from system and cloud backups. Your master password is not stored
and cannot be recovered by anyone.

## Device features, used on the phone only
- **Camera:** scans text or QR codes you point it at. Frames are processed on the device and never stored or sent.
- **Pictures you pick:** read on the device to recognise text, never stored.
- **Bluetooth / nearby devices:** used only when you move your vault to a new phone. The link runs directly
  between your two phones and is end-to-end encrypted. It is never used to determine location.
- **Biometrics:** unlock uses the system fingerprint or face prompt. The app never sees your biometric data.

## Backups and imports
Encrypted backup files and CSV imports are created or read only when you choose to, at a location you pick.

## Deleting your data
Use Settings > Erase vault from this phone, or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so that
removes all data.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
